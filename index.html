Fair questions

Asked, answered, folded.

What if I need something back faster?

Real emergency — an interview, a formal, an incident? DM @cleanyourdirtylaundry and we'll tell you straight whether we can pull it off. Otherwise the rhythm is Tuesday out, Thursday back. Two days, every week, like clockwork.

What if I forget to put the bag out?

We text you a reminder Monday night, every week. The reminder comes to you, the bag goes out Tuesday, and the pile never gets its sequel.

Will my hoodie come back a crop top?

No. Your clothes are washed by people who do this for a living at a commercial facility, not a roommate with a hamper and optimism. Cold wash where it matters, low heat where it counts. Delicates get treated like delicates. The hoodie comes back the size it left.

How much fits in the bag?

20 pounds — a full week of one person's clothes: shirts, pants, gym gear, socks, towels from the shower kind of life. It's a hard cap, and the hard cap is the honest part: it's what lets the price stay flat instead of creeping like everyone else's. If it's bulging like it owes you money, that's a linens conversation — see below.

What about comforters, sheets, and bedding?

Separate, on purpose. The weekly bag is for everyday clothes. Comforters are $20 each as an add-on — tell us at pickup. Sheet and linen bundles are their own add-on, priced when you sign up. Your comforter weighs as much as your entire wardrobe; pretending otherwise is how other services end up with surge weeks.

What happens if my bag is overweight?

We weigh at the facility. A little over, we'll wash it and text you a heads-up. A comforter stuffed in the middle, we'll text you an add-on link before anything gets washed — no surprise charges, ever. Nothing gets billed that you didn't say yes to.

Is $100 a month actually worth it?

Add up machine money, detergent, dryer sheets, and every Sunday between now and May. Then decide what an afternoon of your semester is worth. If your time is worth nothing, no. If it's worth anything at all, yes.

Where do you actually wash it?

A real commercial laundromat on North Raleigh Boulevard. Industrial machines, trained staff, an actual building with an address. Not a side hustle with a basket and a dream, and never a stranger's bathtub.

Can my parents pay?

Yes, and most will want to. It's one flat charge, no surprise fees, and it's the difference between calling home about life and calling home about laundry. Send them the parent page — it was written for them.
