# HAMPER'D trend playbook — riding viral laundry content
(Aug 2 2026. The system behind the fitted-sheet ads, written down so it
repeats. Brand: Hamper'd. Voice: deadpan, dry, no exclamation marks.)

## Why this works for us specifically
Viral chore content is almost always the same joke: a hated chore moment,
anthropomorphized. That is literally our brand universe — The Pile is a
sentient laundry menace, the Bag is the fix. Every laundry-pain trend that
goes viral is a free setup for our punchline: "Let Hamper'd take care of
this mess. So you don't have to."

## The three legal lanes (pick one per trend, never a fourth)
1. **Licensed creator edit** — get the creator's written permission, then
   edit their footage with our outro/tag (e.g. the Miriam J fitted-sheet
   edit). Keep the license on file BEFORE posting; scope it: which
   platforms, organic and/or paid, for how long. Their permission never
   covers third-party music baked into the clip — cut it or strip audio.
2. **Re-film the format** — trends are formats and formats aren't
   copyrighted. One phone, one performer, our twist at the end. Cheapest
   and most native-feeling.
3. **Rebuild in canon** — retell the trend's joke with our art system and
   deadpan type (e.g. the fitted-sheet edition reel). Zero dependencies,
   fully on-brand, reusable forever.
Never lane zero: reposting someone's footage without permission.

## The 48-hour trend-response pipeline
1. **Spot** (daily, 10 min): scroll laundry/cleaning/chore hashtags and
   sounds. A trend qualifies if the pain is laundry-adjacent and the
   format survives a deadpan retelling.
2. **Map**: which canon character owns this joke — the Pile (mess grows),
   the Bag (rescue), the Chair (victim), the fitted sheet (chaos agent)?
3. **Make**: pick a lane above. Re-film or rebuild inside 48h — trends
   decay in about a week.
4. **Finish**: our standard outro (end card + lemon hamperd.com pill) goes
   on everything. Same card every time — that repetition IS the brand.
5. **Sound**: masters ship silent; add audio in-app at post time from the
   platform's COMMERCIAL library (business accounts cannot use regular
   trending sounds in ads).
6. **Caption**: deadpan, short, no hashtag soup — 4 to 6 relevant tags.

## Ready-to-shoot concepts (similar contents, in order)
1. **Fitted-sheet, live-action re-film** — script below. Strongest next post.
2. **POV: the sock that got away** — one sock on the floor behind the
   dryer, slow zoom, caption "It knew." End card.
3. **The Chair** — time-lapse of a chair disappearing under clothes over
   "one week" of jump cuts. Caption "Day 6. The chair is gone." (Reuses
   v1's best joke in live action.)
4. **POV: laundry hears the Hamper'd truck** — pile by the door, truck
   sound outside, pile "flinches" (yank a hidden string). Bag glows on
   the mat.
5. **"It has a face now."** — recreate v1's Day 9 beat in live action:
   hoodie + sunglasses arranged on the pile, dead stare, nothing happens.
6. **Duet/Remix lane** — use Instagram's native Remix on viral laundry
   fails (platform-sanctioned, attribution stays with the creator): our
   side is just the Bag sitting there, glowing, judging.

## Live-action fitted-sheet shoot script (15 s, one phone, one person)
- 0-2 s  Static shot: open dryer, sheet half out. On-screen text:
  "POV: laundry day, week 3" (our text, not the original creator's).
- 2-8 s  Performer inside drum wrapped in a fitted sheet, slowly pulls
  towels in under the sheet, deadpan face to camera.
- 8-12 s Ball forms; performer disappears inside; one hand pats the ball.
- 12-15 s Hard cut: the Hamper'd bag on a doormat. Text: "Send in the bag."
- Attach standard outro card in the edit. Add cleared audio in-app.

## Measurement (weekly, 5 min)
Saves and shares > likes. Watch-through at the outro card is the number
that matters — if people bail before the card, tighten the middle beat.
Two posts a week: one trend-reactive, one canon-lore.
