## Posting checklist
1. Audio: pick one trending lo-fi / deadpan-comedy sound, volume low —
   the type does the talking.
2. Cover: use `hamperd-reel-cover-hook.png` so the grid shows the hook.
3. Pin a comment with the plain link: hamperd.com
4. Do not add platform text overlays on top — the safe zones are already
   respected in the composition.
5. Post windows that fit the joke: Sunday evening or Monday morning
   (laundry dread peaks).

## Voice rails used (same family voice as CYDL, Hamper'd branding)
- Deadpan, dry, short sentences, no exclamation marks.
- No prices, no phone number, no invented reviews/stats.
- Lemon Zest appears only on the CTA pill.
- The Pile: smug-sleepy. The Bag: an object, no face.
