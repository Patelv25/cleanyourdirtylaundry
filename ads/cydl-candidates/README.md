art. Vee ruled (Aug 2 2026) that all of that art is CYDL-only, so they
live here now. They still carry a Hamper'd end card, so they are NOT
postable as-is for either brand:

- `cydl-candidate-pile-story.mp4` (22s) — the "one hoodie → the chair is
  gone → it has a face" story. Dorm footage, Pile art. Reads naturally
  as a CYDL launch ad.
- `cydl-candidate-fitted-sheet.mp4` (17.4s) — fitted-sheet trend
  retelling with the washing machine, night standoff, and fold scenes.

To convert one for CYDL: swap the end card in
`ads/hamperd/src/reel.html` / `reel2.html` to CYDL branding (wordmark,
cleanyourdirtylaundry.com, "Claim your spot" CTA, facts from AGENTS.md
rail #1 only) and re-render. Ask the agent — it is a copy change plus a
five-minute render.

Never post these as Hamper'd.
