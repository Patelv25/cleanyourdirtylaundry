The system

Built so nobody has to remember.

Bag out Tuesday morning. Back Thursday — washed, dried, folded. We text a reminder Monday night, every week, so the schedule survives midterms, formals, and eight hours of sleep debt.

Where the clothes actually go

A commercial laundromat on North Raleigh Boulevard — industrial machines, trained staff, a building with an address.

If the bag is overweight

We weigh at the facility. A little over: we wash it and text a heads-up. A comforter stuffed in the middle: we text an add-on link before anything gets washed. Nothing is ever billed that you didn't say yes to.

How clothes are treated

Cold wash where it matters, low heat where it counts, delicates treated like delicates. The hoodie comes back the size it left. That's a point of professional pride.

If something goes wrong

Email us and a human answers. We're launch-size and founder-run — small enough that one badly handled bag is a personal problem, and it gets fixed like one.
